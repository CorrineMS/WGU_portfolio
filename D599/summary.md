# Course Summary

## D599: Data Preparation and Exploration

**Course Status:** Completed  

D599 focuses on preparing, cleaning, and exploring data to ensure accuracy, consistency, and analytical readiness. The course emphasizes profiling datasets, interpreting descriptive statistics, applying data wrangling techniques, and using visualizations to uncover patterns and data quality issues prior to advanced analysis.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Profiles Data**  
  Interprets data dictionaries and evaluates dataset structure, types, and quality.

- **Interprets Statistics and Visualization**  
  Applies descriptive statistics and visualizations to identify trends, distributions, and anomalies.

- **Wrangles Data**  
  Cleans, transforms, and prepares data to ensure accuracy, format consistency, and integrity.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Task 1: Data Cleaning and Profiling
Analyzed a raw dataset to identify quality issues, reviewed metadata and data dictionaries, and documented dataset characteristics including structure, data types, and observed anomalies.

### Task 2: Data Preparation and Statistical Analysis
Cleaned and transformed data using analytics programming techniques, applied descriptive statistics, and generated summaries to support analytical decision-making.

### Task 3: Advanced Exploration and Pattern Analysis
Performed deeper exploratory analysis to identify relationships and patterns within the dataset, supporting insights relevant to business or organizational questions.
