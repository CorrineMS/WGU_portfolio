# Course Summary

## D602: Advanced Data Analytics

**Course Status:** Completed  

D602 focuses on applying advanced analytical techniques to solve complex, data-driven problems. The course emphasizes selecting appropriate analytical methods, evaluating model performance, and interpreting results to support strategic decision making. Learners develop a deeper understanding of analytical reasoning and methodological rigor.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Selects Appropriate Analytical Methods**  
  Evaluates problem contexts to choose suitable analytical approaches.

- **Applies Advanced Analytical Techniques**  
  Implements methods to analyze complex datasets and extract meaningful insights.

- **Evaluates Analytical Results**  
  Assesses outcomes, assumptions, and limitations to ensure valid conclusions.

- **Communicates Analytical Findings**  
  Clearly explains methods, results, and implications to stakeholders.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Advanced Analytical Problem Solving
Applied advanced analytics techniques to analyze data, interpret results, and support decision-making scenarios. Emphasized methodological accuracy, validation, and clear communication of findings.
