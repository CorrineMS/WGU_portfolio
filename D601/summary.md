# Course Summary

## D601: Data Visualization

**Course Status:** Completed  

D601 focuses on designing and interpreting effective data visualizations to communicate insights clearly and accurately. The course emphasizes selecting appropriate visual encodings, applying visualization best practices, and tailoring visual outputs to support analytical storytelling and decision making.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Selects Appropriate Data Visualizations**  
  Chooses visualization types that accurately represent data and analytical intent.

- **Designs Effective Visual Displays**  
  Applies design principles to create clear, accessible, and meaningful visuals.

- **Interprets Data Visualizations**  
  Analyzes charts and dashboards to identify trends, relationships, and outliers.

- **Communicates Insights Through Visualization**  
  Uses visuals to convey analytical findings to technical and non-technical audiences.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Data Visualization and Insight Communication
Created visualizations to explore and communicate data-driven insights. Applied design best practices to ensure clarity, accuracy, and relevance while supporting analytical narratives.
