# Course Summary

## D604: Advanced Data Visualization and Communication

**Course Status:** Completed  

D604 builds on foundational visualization skills by focusing on advanced data visualization techniques and analytical communication. The course emphasizes designing compelling visual narratives, tailoring visualizations to stakeholder needs, and clearly communicating complex analytical insights to support decision making.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Designs Advanced Data Visualizations**  
  Applies advanced visualization techniques to represent complex data effectively.

- **Evaluates Visualization Effectiveness**  
  Assesses clarity, accuracy, and usability of visual displays.

- **Communicates Analytical Insights**  
  Translates analytical findings into clear, audience-appropriate visual narratives.

- **Supports Data-Driven Decision Making**  
  Uses visualizations to inform strategic and operational decisions.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Advanced Visualization and Insight Communication
Developed advanced visualizations and analytical narratives to communicate insights derived from complex datasets. Emphasized clarity, design best practices, and alignment with stakeholder objectives.
