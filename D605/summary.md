# Course Summary

## D605: Advanced Analytics Applications

**Course Status:** Completed  

D605 focuses on applying advanced analytics techniques to real-world business and organizational problems. The course emphasizes integrating analytical methods, evaluating outcomes, and communicating results in a professional and actionable manner.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Integrates Advanced Analytical Methods**  
  Combines analytical techniques to address complex data-driven scenarios.

- **Evaluates Analytical Outcomes**  
  Assesses results, assumptions, and limitations to ensure valid conclusions.

- **Applies Analytics to Business Contexts**  
  Uses analytical findings to support organizational decision making.

- **Communicates Results Professionally**  
  Presents insights clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Applied Advanced Analytics Project
Applied advanced analytics techniques to analyze data and generate actionable insights. Focused on methodological rigor, evaluation of results, and professional communication of findings.
