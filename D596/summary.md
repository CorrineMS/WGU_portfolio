# Course Summary

## D596: The Data Analytics Journey

**Course Status:** Completed  
**Course Dates:** August 4, 2025 – August 27, 2025  

D596 introduces the data analytics life cycle and explores career pathways within the analytics field. The course emphasizes applying analytics concepts to real-world business scenarios while developing foundational skills in analytics planning, ethical considerations, and professional self-assessment.

---

## Course Objectives

WGU outlines the following competencies as part of this course:

- **Applies the Data Analytics Life Cycle**  
  Applies the seven phases of the data analytics life cycle to business scenarios.

- **Proposes a Data Analytics Plan**  
  Develops an analytics plan aligned with organizational goals and requirements.

- **Differentiates Careers and Roles**  
  Evaluates analytics roles and career paths across multiple disciplines.

- **Identifies Potential Career Goals**  
  Establishes career goals based on strengths, interests, and analytics pathways.

---

## Practical Assessments

### Task 1: The Data Analytics Life Cycle
Focused on explaining each phase of the analytics life cycle, identifying tools and risks, and evaluating ethical considerations.

### Task 2: Careers and Career Goals
Developed a data analytics career plan based on industry research, labor statistics, and CliftonStrengths results.

---

## Key Skills Demonstrated

- Analytics lifecycle understanding  
- Business problem framing  
- Ethical evaluation in analytics  
- Career planning and professional reflection
